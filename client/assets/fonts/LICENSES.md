## IBM VGA 8x16 (`ibm-vga-8x16.woff`)

The text font of the game: "WebPlus IBM VGA 8x16" from The Ultimate Oldschool PC Font Pack, version 2.2, by VileR (https://int10h.org/oldschool-pc-fonts/).

## Black Ops One (`black-ops-one.woff2`)

The font of the name of the game: Black Ops One by Sorkin Type (https://github.com/SorkinType/Black-Ops), the Latin subset as packaged by Fontsource.
